1. Renew your license online

Goal: Can I renew online and where do I start?

You may be able to renew a standard driver's license or ID card online.

Use the online renewal option to start the renewal on this page.

Check timing Online renewal is possible within 90 days before your expiration date, or up to 12 months after it expires.
  • You cannot renew online if you are applying for a REAL ID for the first time. That needs an office visit.
  • If you did not get a renewal notice, you can request a copy or sign up for paperless renewal notices.
Start the renewal online If you must visit an office

2. Renew at a DMV office

Goal: How to renew in person and save time

You can renew a standard driver's license in person at a DMV office.

Renew at a DMV office if your renewal notice tells you to renew there.

  • Bring the DMV renewal notice mailed to you, or complete a driver license or ID card application.
  • At the office, have your thumbprint scanned, pass a vision exam, and have your photo taken.
  • Save time at your office visit by starting the renewal online first.

3. What documents do I need for a REAL ID?

Goal: Gather the right papers before you visit

Bring proof of identity and proof of California residency.

  • Prove your identity with one document such as a valid U.S. passport or a birth certificate.
  • Prove California residency with documents such as a utility bill or a bank statement.
  • A REAL ID application accounts for two residency documents — you need a second residency document as well.
  • Use the interactive REAL ID document checklist to gather the documents you need.
What to bring Complete the application online, then bring the original documents and your confirmation code to the office visit.

4. How to schedule an appointment

Goal: Get help at an office without extra trips

To make an appointment, choose a service such as renewing a driver license or ID with changes or an in-person requirement.

Begin the appointment process with the Start Application step.

Know Before You Go The Know Before You Go notice says counter service is not offered for license renewal unless your notice says so. Check your notice before you book.
  • An existing appointment can be reviewed or cancelled through View Appointment.
  • Check office wait times before you go to the DMV.

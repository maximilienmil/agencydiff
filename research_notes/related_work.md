# Related work

*README-ready. ~260 words. The full, sceptical version — including what we must not claim — is in [positioning.md](positioning.md).*

Dark-pattern research has mostly measured two things: whether a snapshot contains a manipulative pattern, and whether an agent falls for one. Screenshot and mobile detectors classify single UIs (AidUI, [arXiv:2303.06782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06782); UIGuard, [arXiv:2308.05898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.05898); AutoBot, [arXiv:2411.07441](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07441)); UMBRA traces consent flows at web scale ([arXiv:2603.21515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21515)). Susceptibility benchmarks instead test the agent: SusBench injects patterns into live sites ([arXiv:2510.11035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11035)), DECEPTICON reports patterns steering agents in over 70% of tasks ([arXiv:2512.22894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22894)), TrickyArena finds ~41% susceptibility to one pattern ([arXiv:2510.18113](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18113)), and LLM agents now audit live rights-request flows at scale ([arXiv:2603.03881](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03881)). Closest structurally, AgentA/B runs persona agents on two live variants and compares population behaviour ([arXiv:2504.09723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09723)); UXAgent simulates usability studies ([arXiv:2504.09407](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09407)); intent-driven GUI testers pursue coverage on one build ([arXiv:2311.08649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08649)); visual regression diffs pixels, a comparison recent work calls "semantically blind" ([arXiv:2607.01728](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01728)).

AgencyDiff holds one paired Intent — subscribe vs cancel — fixed, varies only the product version, and reduces recorded traces to deterministic deltas: action cost, navigation depth, initial visibility, default, and accept-vs-protective asymmetry, with success decided by final app state, not the agent's claim. The metrics are law-shaped: removing a first-layer reject raised consent by 22–23 points ([arXiv:2001.02479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02479)), and DSA Article 25 prohibits manipulative interface design on EU platforms ([Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj)). The result is not a detection score or a simulated-user study. It is a regression test for a product promise, delivered pre-merge with replayable evidence.

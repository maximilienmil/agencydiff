Online Renewal

Short answer: Many standard renewals can be completed online. Start in the Online Renewal area of the official agency site.

Check first

  • Your renewal notice says if you are eligible to renew online.
  • Have your license number, date of birth, and renewal notice nearby.
  • If you need a new photo, test, or REAL ID upgrade, you may be asked to come in.

Steps

  1. Confirm you are eligible for online renewal.
  2. Verify your name and address are current.
  3. Follow the official online instructions to submit your renewal.

Renew in Person at an Office

Short answer: You can renew at a local office instead of online. To save time on your office visit, bring your notice and documents and complete any available pre-application worksheet in advance.

How it works

  1. Choose an office location and time that works for you.
  2. Bring your renewal notice, current license, and any documents listed for your renewal type.
  3. Check in on arrival and follow staff directions for photo and review.

Tip for a shorter office visit: double-check your papers the night before and arrive a little early for your time slot.

REAL ID Documents

Short answer: A REAL ID renewal needs extra identity papers. Gather your documents before you apply or book an office time.

Gather your documents

Bring originals or certified copies. As a general guide, people are asked for:

  • One document showing identity and date of birth, such as a passport or birth certificate.
  • One document showing Social Security number, such as a Social Security card or tax form.
  • Two documents showing California residency, such as mail with your name and address.

This is a summary only. Use the official REAL ID checklist to confirm which papers are accepted, and bring extras if a name has changed.

Appointments

Short answer: Appointments are scheduled through the official appointment system. Choose service type, location, and time, then save your confirmation.

How to schedule

  1. Select “driver’s license renewal” as the service reason.
  2. Enter your ZIP or county to see nearby offices.
  3. Pick a date and time and save the confirmation details.

If no times are available, try another nearby office or check back later. You can reschedule or cancel with your confirmation information.

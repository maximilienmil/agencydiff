1. Can I renew my California driver's license online?

Yes — most drivers can renew online. Start in MyDMV with Online Driver's License Renewal.

Use your MyDMV account to begin the online renewal. The online service will walk you through eligibility questions and your current license information.

How to start

  1. Go to the MyDMV online services section in this guide.
  2. Choose Driver's License Renewal.
  3. Follow the prompts to confirm your identity and submit.

Some renewals still require an in-person office visit, for example if a new photo, test, or identity check is needed. The online service will tell you if you need to come in.

2. Renew at a DMV office instead of online

You can renew in person at any DMV office. Walk in or use Get in Line. To save time, complete the online application before you visit.

If you prefer not to renew online, visit a DMV office for driver's license renewal. Staff can help with photos, tests, and REAL ID upgrades.

How to save time on your office visit

  • Fill out the online driver's license application at home before you go.
  • Use Get in Line Now on the day of your visit, if available at that office.
  • Bring your current license and any letters or notices you received.
  • Bring your REAL ID documents if you want to upgrade at the same visit.

3. What documents do I need for a REAL ID?

Bring three types of documents: proof of identity, proof of Social Security number, and two proofs of California residency.

  • 1. Proof of identity — such as a valid U.S. passport or a certified birth certificate.
  • 2. Proof of Social Security number — such as a Social Security card or W-2 showing the full number.
  • 3. Two proofs of California residency — such as a utility bill, rental agreement, or bank statement with your California address.

Bring original documents or certified copies. Photocopies and phone images are generally not accepted. If your name has changed, bring name-change documents as well.

4. How to schedule an appointment at a DMV office

Use the online appointment system to Make an Appointment. Choose your office, service, and date and time.

  1. Open the online appointment service for DMV offices.
  2. Choose Driver's License or Renewal as the service type.
  3. Select an office location and an available date and time.
  4. Save your confirmation and bring it with your documents.

An appointment is different from Get in Line. An appointment is booked for a future date and time. Get in Line is for same-day visits.
